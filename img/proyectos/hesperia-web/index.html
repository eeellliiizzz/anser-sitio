What We Do

Three
transformations.

We take dated 1990s and 2000s Florida homes and give them the warmth of a European villa — without touching the structure that makes them sound.

01 / 03
01

Interiors · Warm Minimalism

Dark woods, brown granite, heavy columns and yellowed walls give way to calm. We keep the classic soul and edit everything else — smooth lime plaster and microcement in limestone and bone, sandblasted beams returned to natural oak, false arches removed to open the plan to the light.

Lime plaster & microcement Matte limestone & travertine Wide-plank European oak Open, light-filled plans

02

Façade Refinement

The architecture stays; the excess goes. Foam moldings, faux corbels and resin ornament are stripped away. In their place: slender wrought iron, Spanish-inspired lanterns and a grand arched entry door in solid oak or teak — fully impact-rated to Florida code.

Ornament removal Hand-forged iron & lighting Arched impact wood doors

Resort Outdoor Living

In South Florida, you buy the climate. Our backyards are where the return lives — coral-stone and handmade terracotta terraces that stay cool underfoot, sculptural Mediterranean planting adapted to the tropics, washed-oak pergolas and outdoor kitchens finished in microcement and zellige.
